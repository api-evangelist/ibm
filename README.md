IBM offers solutions that protect identities and sensitive access for enterprise customers. Its IBM Vault® product is highlighted as a tool for identity protection and regulatory compliance, and IBM hosts webinars on trust, identity, and governance in the AI era. The company targets organizations seeking secure identity management and access control across their systems.

**Areas:** Database · IBM · API Management · Artificial Intelligence · Billing · Cloud Computing · Containers · Data Governance · DevOps · Enterprise · Generative AI · Hybrid Cloud

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for IBM: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
